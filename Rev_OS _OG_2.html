What we found

Tender → Award conversion is 31% against a 36% target, and average tender age is 74 days.

Across Propose-stage pursuit conversations, the weakest recurring behavior is Impact Quantification. Pursuit teams are documenting the operator’s well program and drilling challenges, but they are not consistently quantifying:

  • NPT avoided and uptime delivered
  • Production uplift and cost per barrel
  • The operator’s measure of success

Recommended action

Tune Q3 Tender Conversion Sprint

Activate the Impact Quantification Playbook in cX Dialogue Copilot across Propose-stage pursuit conversations for the next 30 days.

Dialogue Copilot will prompt pursuit teams to quantify operator impact during the conversation and capture the confirmed information in Dynamics 365.

How we will measure it

  • Impact quantification coverage
  • Average tender age
  • Tender → Award conversion
